### CH21-P024

Translation:

Chung Ju-yung often assigned work to his subordinates on the basis of seemingly sudden judgments. Yet those sudden judgments contained both singular insight and precise calculation. Lee Byung-chull believed that large enterprises must sometimes rise above profit. He once put it this way:

### CH21-P029
