Putting talent first and people at the center has been my creed for many years, as well as Samsung's management philosophy and supporting pillar.

## CH20-P002

作为一个企业家，应该不遗余力地培养人才。如果企业家对于人才的期待和渴望能够传达到每一个职员那里的话，这个企业便走上了无限繁荣的道路。

### English Translation

An entrepreneur should spare no effort in developing people. If the entrepreneur's expectations and desire for talent reach every employee, the enterprise will enter a path of limitless prosperity.

## CH20-P004

### 中文原文

善于用人的企业家，他们看人的感觉是与众不同的。在这一点上，李秉哲和郑周永各不相同。

### English Translation

Entrepreneurs skilled at employing people possess a distinctive sense for judging them. Lee Byung-chull and Chung Ju-yung differed in how they did so.

## CH20-P005

### 中文原文

李秉哲强调的是奖惩分明。“经营者是什么？是干事业的人。有人把经营者和君子混为一谈。经营者虽然具备实力，但是还要有德望和权威。而君子是具有德行。这会在奖惩分明的时候体现出来。比如说发生事故的时候，是由于出于好意才出现的，还是由于办事不力出现的，还是由于偶然性的因素发生的。如果是经过仔细考察，想把事情做好却以失败告终，这也是需要嘉奖的。因为失误发生事故的话则需要惩罚。”

### English Translation

Lee emphasized clear rewards and punishments. “What is a manager? Someone who conducts an enterprise. Some confuse a manager with a gentleman. A manager must possess ability, moral standing, and authority, while a gentleman possesses virtue. The difference appears in the clear administration of rewards and punishments. When an accident occurs, for example, was it caused by good intentions, by incompetence, or by chance? If someone investigated carefully and tried to do the job well but failed, that person should still be commended. An accident caused by negligence must be punished.”

## CH20-P006

### 中文原文

由于李秉哲奉行赏罚分明的原则，所以三星成了人才的聚集地。他在谈到评价人才的标准时说过这样的话：“人会由于能力的变化而改变。能力也会提高。虽然说可以通过教育提高能力，但不是说谁都可以做到的。能否努力是一种天生的素质，人的发展取决于是否具备这种素质。所以比起教育本身，对人自身素质的评价更为重要。”

### English Translation

Because Lee followed this principle, Samsung became a gathering place for talent. Discussing evaluation, he said, “People change as their abilities change, and abilities can improve. Education can raise ability, but not everyone can do so. The capacity for effort is an innate quality, and a person's development depends on possessing it. Evaluating the person's own qualities is therefore more important than education itself.”

## CH20-P007

### 中文原文

在工作中，如果李秉哲认可，他就会安排很多事情给这个人，使得他有充分发挥自己才能的机会。

### English Translation

When Lee recognized someone's ability, he assigned that person many tasks, providing ample opportunity to display it.

## CH20-P008

### 中文原文

在开发中央处理器（CPU）的时候，担任项目的常务意识到开发的重要性，向李秉哲进行讲解说明。核心思想是如果要开发的话需要投入1000万美元的巨款，但是没有一定能够成功的信心。

### English Translation

During development of a central processing unit, the managing director responsible for the project explained its importance to Lee. Development would require an enormous investment of 10 million dollars, but success could not be guaranteed.

## CH20-P009

### 中文原文

“停一下。你可是一个常务啊！哪怕1000万白白地丢了，你作为公司的中坚，也是可以放手一干的。我从来不记得曾把一个连自信都没有的人委任为常务的。”

### English Translation

“Stop. You are a managing director! Even if the entire ten million is lost, as a pillar of the company you should be able to proceed boldly. I do not recall ever appointing someone without confidence as a managing director.”

## CH20-P010

### 中文原文

还有一件发生在“第一制糖”的事情。当时对于制糖原料的征税急剧上升，公司蒙受了数以亿计的损失。征税上调，这不是任何人的过错，李秉哲把这种无法预测的事情看作是经营的一个失误。可是，他对此却没有追究任何人的失职。

### English Translation

Another incident occurred at Cheil Sugar. A sharp increase in taxes on sugar-refining materials cost the company hundreds of millions of won. No individual was at fault for the tax increase, but Lee regarded the failure to anticipate it as a management error. Even so, he held no one responsible.

## CH20-P011

### 中文原文

可是，负责的职员承担起责任，向公司递交了辞职信。这时，李秉哲把那位职员叫来，安慰他：“蒙受了数以亿计的损失，我很难过，但是这同看到你勇于承担责任使我感到欣慰相比，就不算什么了。”

### English Translation

The employee in charge nevertheless accepted responsibility and submitted his resignation. Lee summoned and comforted him: “The loss of hundreds of millions saddens me, but it is nothing compared with the pleasure I feel at seeing you take responsibility so courageously.”

## CH20-P012

### 中文原文

### English Translation

Lee not only returned the resignation but promoted the employee.

## CH20-P014

### English Translation

From then on, Lee earned Chung's appreciation and began a brilliant rise. He became Hyundai Construction's managing director for administration in 1972, senior managing director in 1974, and president in 1975 at the age of thirty-five. In only ten years after joining the company in 1965, he rose from an ordinary employee to president.

## CH20-P015

### English Translation

## CH20-P016

### 中文原文

他选用的标准是学历 50 分，人品 50 分。在人品中，需要相貌端正，身体健康，富有创造力。这一点和那些注重笔试分数的企业相比是完全不同的。

### English Translation

His selection standard assigned fifty points to education and fifty to personal character. Character included a respectable appearance, physical health, and creativity. This differed entirely from companies focused on written-examination scores.

## CH20-P017

### 中文原文

“不管头脑有多聪明，人品不行也是不行的。而且，我认为好的人品是通过端正的相貌体现出来的。另外，还需要有进取心，需要性格开朗、活跃。此外，还需要不满足于现状，能够不断进步和努力的健康体魄。”

### English Translation

“No matter how intelligent a person is, poor character makes him unsuitable. I also believe good character is reflected in a proper appearance. A person needs initiative and a cheerful, active disposition, as well as a healthy body that is never satisfied with the status quo and can continue advancing and striving.”

## CH20-P018

### 中文原文

李秉哲凭借着这些标准，大部分时候都可以挑选出优秀人才。

### English Translation

Using these standards, Lee selected outstanding people in most cases.

## CH20-P019

### 中文原文

郑周永也是如此，比起经历和学历来，更重视人的品行。“做一些事情的时候不是依靠经验和学历，而是靠着一个人的品德和姿态。遇到困难的时候，为了解决问题，就需要付出全部的努力。不存在什么无法解决的理由。对于那些勤奋的人来说，那些理由根本就称不上理由。”

### English Translation

Chung likewise valued character more than experience or education. “Accomplishing something depends not on experience or schooling but on a person's character and attitude. When difficulties arise, resolving them requires total effort. There is no valid reason why a problem cannot be solved. For diligent people, such reasons are no reasons at all.”

## CH20-P020

### 中文原文

所以，郑周永评判、选择人才的时候看重的是能够努力。努力主要表现在对做事的热情。拥有热情的人才会不断努力，最终实现自己的目标。

### English Translation

Chung therefore valued the capacity for effort when judging and selecting people. Effort appeared chiefly as passion for the task. People with passion continued striving until they achieved their goals.

## CH20-P021

### 中文原文

“可以从做一件小事看出一个人的诚实，那么我们也有理由相信他在做大事的时候也会同样诚实。我们可以相信一个不违背小诺言的人也会不违背大的诺言。由此我们可以相信：一个人做小事都会全力以赴的话，做大事也会全力以赴。那就是所谓的信用。”

### English Translation

“A person's honesty in a small matter gives us reason to believe he will be equally honest in a large one. We can trust that someone who keeps a minor promise will also keep a major promise. Likewise, a person who gives his utmost to a small task will do the same for a great undertaking. That is what credibility means.”

## CH20-P022

### 中文原文

郑周永信奉信用第一主义。只有那些诚实、守信、全力以赴去做所有事的人，才是他选择的对象。

### English Translation

Chung placed credibility above all else. He chose only those who were honest, trustworthy, and gave their utmost to every task.
