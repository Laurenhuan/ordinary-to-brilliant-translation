# Chapter 1 Cultural Concept Review

Stage: 5A.6 — Human Approval Consolidation

Scope: formal consolidation of the seven cultural-concept decisions approved by the project lead after Stage 5A.5. These decisions are authoritative for Chapter 1 translation preparation. No Chapter 1 prose has been translated, and the frozen Chinese source remains unchanged.

## Approved Chapter 1 cultural-concept policy

1. A Korean historical education institution may retain a Korean term when a modern generic English label would mislead. Use the approved first-use explanation and the retained term thereafter.
2. Confucian philosophical terms must preserve their conceptual context and approved relationships. Do not replace them mechanically with easy modern near-synonyms.
3. For Chinese or Korean traditional sayings, prioritize natural English readability while preserving the intended cultural meaning. Use established English equivalents when appropriate and record every substitution decision.
4. A proverb/quotation decision need not be treated as a glossary entity when human approval explicitly says otherwise. Its candidate record and QA decision remain authoritative.

## CULT-CH01-001 — 私塾

- **Chinese term:** 私塾
- **Context:** CH01-P006, P007, P013, P015, P016, and P024; the source describes the traditional education received by Lee Byung-chull and Chung Ju-yung, including instruction in classical Chinese texts.
- **Approved translation:** First occurrence: `a traditional Korean village school known as a seodang`. Later occurrences: `seodang`.
- **Alternative translation:** `private school` is explicitly prohibited.
- **Cultural explanation:** Classified as a Korean historical education institution. Retaining `seodang` preserves the cultural setting, while the first-use explanation supplies reader orientation without suggesting a modern private school.
- **Recommendation:** Apply the approved first-use/later-use rule exactly and coordinate the retained term with `文山亭书院 / Munsanjeong Seodang` when that separate candidate is approved.
- **Human decision:** Approved
- **Status:** `locked`

## CULT-CH01-002 — 鸿儒

- **Cultural explanation:** Classified as a cultural concept. The approved wording preserves both intellectual distinction and the Confucian setting.
- **Recommendation:** Apply `eminent Confucian scholar` for the Chapter 1 occurrence.
- **Human decision:** Approved
- **Status:** `locked`

## CULT-CH01-003 — 地主

- **Chinese term:** 地主
- **Context:** CH01-P005 and P006; Chapter 1 describes landholding persons and families and their harvest capacity.
- **Approved translation:** Person: `landowner`. Family: `landowning family`.
- **Alternative translation:** `landlord` is explicitly prohibited.
- **Cultural explanation:** `Landlord` commonly suggests a rental-property relationship in modern English, which is not established by these source occurrences.
- **Recommendation:** Apply the approved grammatical, context-sensitive pair; do not use one form as a blind global replacement for both contexts.
- **Human decision:** Approved
- **Status:** `locked`

## CULT-CH01-004 — 仁、和、乐

- **Chinese term:** 仁、和、乐
- **Context:** CH01-P025 and P026; the source presents the three terms together as the spiritual or philosophical realm described by the *Analects*.
- **Approved translation:** First occurrence: `humaneness (ren), harmony (he), and joy (le)`. Later occurrences: `humaneness`, `harmony`, and `joy`.
- **Alternative translation:** Mechanical substitution with `kindness / peace / happiness` is explicitly prohibited.
- **Cultural explanation:** The approved gloss-plus-transliteration form preserves the Confucian philosophical context and the three-part relationship.
- **Recommendation:** Apply the approved first-use form exactly; use the approved English glosses without parenthetical transliterations on later occurrences.
- **Human decision:** Approved
- **Status:** `locked`
