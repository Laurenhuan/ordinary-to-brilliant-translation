## CTX-CH01-001 — 文山亭书院

- **Chinese term:** 文山亭书院
- **Type:** school / Korean historical educational institution
- **Chapter segment:** CH01-P014 (FB-P015)
- **Historical context:** The source describes study at an institution opened by the subject's grandfather. Academy of Korean Studies references treat `seodang` as a local private educational institution and use `seodang (village school)` in English explanatory material.
- **Candidate translation:** Munsanjeong Seodang (provisional)
- **Alternatives considered:** Munsanjeong Village School; Munsanjeong Seodang School; Munsanjeong Academy
- **Evidence/source:** https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0027680 ; https://www.aks.ac.kr/ikorea/upload/intl/korean/UserFiles/Infokorea_2019_eng.pdf
- **Recommendation:** Retain `Seodang` as a Korean historical institution term. Do not mechanically translate `书院` as `Academy`. Verify the underlying Korean name and approve the first-use explanation before locking.
- **Confidence:** Medium for the `seodang` strategy; lower for the exact institution romanization until the Korean form is confirmed.
- **Context dependency:** High; linked to the project decision for `私塾` and first-use/later-use treatment.
- **Human decision:** Pending
- **Status:** `needs_human_decision`

## CTX-CH01-003 — 东和旅馆

- **Chinese term:** 东和旅馆
- **Type:** organization / lodging establishment
- **Chapter segment:** CH01-P019 (FB-P016)
- **Historical context:** The source places the establishment in 1894 Shanghai. Historical records describe a Japanese inn or ryokan using the form `同和洋行 / Towa Yoko`, which does not map mechanically to the frozen Chinese form `东和旅馆`.
- **Candidate translation:** Donghwa Inn (provisional reader-facing rendering)
- **Alternatives considered:** Towa Yoko; Towa Yoko (Donghwa Inn); Donghwa Hotel
- **Evidence/source:** https://db.history.go.kr/diachronic/level.do?levelId=sa_026r_0020_0030_0120 ; https://anthony.sogang.ac.kr/transactions/VOL89/FullText.pdf
- **Recommendation:** Prefer `Inn` over `Hotel` for the historical lodging context, but do not lock the proper name until a human reviewer resolves the relation between `东和旅馆` and the attested `同和洋行 / Towa Yoko` form. Do not change the frozen source.
- **Confidence:** Low because the entity-name mapping remains unresolved.
- **Context dependency:** High.
- **Human decision:** Pending
- **Status:** `needs_human_decision`

## CTX-PREP-001 — 会长

- **Chinese term:** 会长 (Korean corporate context: 회장)
- **Type:** title_role / project-level preparation term
- **Chapter segment:** Not present in the frozen Chapter 1 source.
- **Historical context:** In Korean company usage, `회장` denotes a senior corporate leadership title and must not be confused with the chair of a meeting or association. Official Hyundai materials distinguish chairman-level and president-level roles.
- **Candidate translation:** chairman; `corporate chairman` may be used as an explanatory classification
- **Alternatives considered:** chair; chairperson; executive chairman; meeting chairman
- **Evidence/source:** https://www.hyundai.com/worldwide/en/newsroom/detail/euisun-chung-inaugurated-as-chairman-of-hyundai-motor-group%252C-opening-a-new-chapter-in-history-0000000611 ; https://www.hyundai.com/content/dam/hyundai/ww/en/images/company/sustainability/about-sustainability/hmc-2022-sustainability-report-en-v9.pdf
- **Recommendation:** Use `chairman` in a corporate title/name context. Use `executive chairman`, `chairperson`, or a CEO formulation only when the relevant entity and period support that exact title.
- **Confidence:** High for the corporate sense; Medium for the exact English title without entity-period evidence.
- **Context dependency:** High; entity, period, official title, and sentence function must be checked.
- **Human decision:** Pending
- **Status:** `needs_human_decision`

## CTX-PREP-002 — 社长

- **Chinese term:** 社长 (Korean corporate context: 사장)
- **Type:** title_role / project-level preparation term
- **Chapter segment:** Not present in the frozen Chapter 1 source.
- **Historical context:** The Korean Basic Dictionary defines `사장 (社長)` as a person who represents a company and is responsible for corporate affairs, with English senses `president; chief executive`. Korean corporate materials distinguish president-level roles from chairman-level roles.
- **Candidate translation:** president; `corporate president` may be used as an explanatory classification
- **Alternatives considered:** president & CEO; chief executive; company president; society president
- **Evidence/source:** https://krdict.korean.go.kr/eng/dicMarinerSearch/search?mainSearchWord=%EC%82%AC%EC%9E%A5 ; https://www.hyundai.com/content/dam/hyundai/ww/en/images/company/sustainability/about-sustainability/hmc-2022-sustainability-report-en-v9.pdf
- **Recommendation:** Use `president` as the default candidate in corporate contexts. Use `President & CEO` or `chief executive` only when the source or the relevant organization's official material establishes CEO authority. Never render it as `society president` merely from the Chinese characters.
- **Confidence:** High for the corporate sense; Medium for the exact title without entity-period evidence.
- **Context dependency:** High. In ordinary Korean corporate usage, chairman is senior to president, but title alone must not be used to infer exact legal authority.
- **Human decision:** Pending
- **Status:** `needs_human_decision`

## CTX-CH01-006 — 石

- **Chinese term:** 石
- **Type:** unit / traditional Korean grain-volume unit
- **Chapter segment:** CH01-P005 (FB-P015)
- **Historical context:** The unit quantifies harvests of 2,000 and 1,500 `石`. Korean reference materials use `seok` as a unit of volume for grain rather than a weight called `stone`.
- **Candidate translation:** seok, with a first-use gloss such as `a traditional Korean unit of grain volume`
- **Alternatives considered:** a numerical conversion; bushels; stone; untranslated Chinese `shi`
- **Evidence/source:** https://www.aks.ac.kr/ikorea/upload/intl/korean/UserFiles/Infokorea_2018_eng.pdf ; https://www.oecd-ilibrary.org/a-new-rural-development-paradigm-for-developing-countries-in-the-21st-century_5jm23m8s3htj.pdf?itemId=%2Fcontent%2Fcomponent%2F9789264252271-12-en&mimeType=pdf
- **Recommendation:** Retain `seok` and explain it briefly on first use. Do not translate it as `stone`. Do not add a precise conversion unless a period-specific conversion policy is researched and approved. Preserve the source numbers exactly.
- **Confidence:** High for the unit type and transliteration strategy; Medium for the final gloss wording.
- **Context dependency:** High; value and conversion can vary by historical context.
- **Human decision:** Pending
- **Status:** `needs_human_decision`
