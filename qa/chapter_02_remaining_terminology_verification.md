## Special Discrepancy A — Namihei Odaira / Konosuke Matsushita

Affected locked candidate: `C2-T003`

Source locations: CH02-P069–CH02-P073, especially P072–P073

Risk: **High**

### Source state

The frozen source names `小源浪平`, gives the dates `1894–1989`, describes fourth-grade schooling as his entire education, places him in poverty and a bicycle shop, and says he moved into the electrical industry and laid the foundation for Hitachi Group.

### External evidence

- Hitachi identifies Namihei Odaira as **1874–1951**, raised by education-minded parents, university-educated in electrical engineering, and founder of Hitachi.
- Panasonic identifies Konosuke Matsushita as **1894–1989**, records his departure from school in the fourth-grade period, apprenticeship in a bicycle shop, later electrical work, and founding of the Matsushita/Panasonic business.

### Verification conclusion

The P072–P073 dates and early-life sequence align strongly with Konosuke Matsushita, while the source assigns them to the printed Namihei Odaira identity and Hitachi. This is an entity-level biographical conflation, not an OCR issue and not merely the previously recorded `小源浪平` / `小平浪平` character discrepancy.

### Required human decision

- Main translation must continue to say `Namihei Odaira` and preserve the source dates and narrative.
- Approve a neutral translator/editor note explaining that Hitachi identifies Namihei Odaira as 1874–1951 and university-educated, while Panasonic's official history associates the 1894–1989 dates and bicycle-shop/electrical-business sequence with Konosuke Matsushita.
- Decide whether this expanded note appears at P069, at the date in P072, or is split into a short first-reference note plus a P072 biographical note.
- Do not change the frozen source, replace the main-text identity with Konosuke Matsushita, or state that the author personally caused the conflation.

Status: `needs_human_decision`

## Special Discrepancy C — Turtle-ship Banknote Narrative

Affected candidate: `C2-T050`

Source location: CH02-P064

Risk: **Medium**

HD Hyundai's official history says the 500-won banknote bearing the turtle ship, together with a photograph of Mipo Bay, played a symbolic role in persuading British banks, guarantors, and shipowners to approve the shipyard project. The frozen source compresses this into obtaining an order worth tens of millions of dollars for establishing a shipyard while holding the banknote.

Recommended policy:

- Preserve the source's compressed narrative in the main translation.
- Use a neutral term such as `a banknote bearing an image of a turtle ship` after human approval.
- Decide whether the difference is material enough for a translator/editor note. If a note is used, distinguish project financing, guarantees, shipowner approval, and orders without rewriting the source sentence.

Status: `needs_human_decision`
