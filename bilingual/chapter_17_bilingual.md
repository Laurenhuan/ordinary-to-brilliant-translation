# Chapter 17 Bilingual Edition

English chapter title: Think Before Acting and Act Before Thinking

## CH17-P001

### 中文原文

把“事业”看成是围棋的话，李秉哲是布局的高手。他从一开始就设计好企业的蓝图一步步前进，很少有大的失误。

![image](../raw/full_book/part_001_200/images/image_037.jpg)

### English Translation

If business were the game of Go, Lee Byung-chull would be a master of opening strategy. He designed the blueprint for an enterprise from the outset and advanced step by step, rarely making a major mistake.

## CH17-P002

### 中文原文

而郑周永则是那种从一开始就进攻的类型。他喜欢冒险。胜则完胜，败则惨败。

### English Translation

Chung Ju-yung, by contrast, was the type who attacked from the beginning. He enjoyed taking risks: when he won, he won completely; when he lost, he lost disastrously.

## CH17-P003

### 中文原文

布局高手李秉哲基础牢靠，而进攻高手郑周永后劲十足。

### English Translation

Lee, the master strategist, built on a firm foundation; Chung, the master attacker, gathered tremendous strength as he advanced.

## CH17-P004

### 中文原文

所以从事业上来看，李秉哲很少有失败的时候。而郑周永初期显得力量薄弱，随着时间的推移则越来越强，直至成功。

### English Translation

In business, Lee therefore seldom failed. Chung appeared weak at first, but grew stronger over time until he achieved success.

## CH17-P005

### 中文原文

李秉哲在经营企业过程中凡事都喜欢做周详的事前调查，之后再开始。而郑周永，正如在朱拜勒港工程中体现出来的，他倾向于用独一无二的想像力和突破力、超强的魄力实现自己的目标。

### English Translation

In managing enterprises, Lee preferred to conduct exhaustive advance research before beginning anything. Chung, as the Jubail Port project demonstrated, tended to achieve his goals through singular imagination, the power to break through barriers, and exceptional drive.

## CH17-P006

### 中文原文

李秉哲比起想像力来，更倾向于逻辑思维能力，比起突破力来更倾向于慎重。

### English Translation

Lee leaned more toward logical thought than imagination, and more toward caution than forceful breakthroughs.

## CH17-P007

### 中文原文

1975 年 5 月，李秉哲在《首尔经济新闻》上的“商界回眸”栏目发表文章，总结自己办企业的心得。

### English Translation

In May 1975, Lee published an article in the “Looking Back on Business” column of the *Seoul Economic Daily*, summarizing what he had learned from running enterprises.

## CH17-P008

### 中文原文

“办企业的着眼点首先要放在国家和人民是否需要，之后再考虑收益、资金、人力、技术等，做自己力所能及的事情。”

### English Translation

“The first consideration in starting a business must be whether the nation and its people need it. Only then should one consider profits, capital, personnel, technology, and other factors, and undertake what lies within one's capabilities.”

## CH17-P009

### 中文原文

这就是李秉哲的企业观。就像他自己说的一样，在开始做事之前总是喜欢深思熟虑，反复考察。制好了蓝图之后再去实现。他勾勒企业前景的能力是非凡的。他知道如果蓝图不设计好，企业的整体都会受到影响的。

### English Translation

This was Lee's view of enterprise. As he said, before beginning anything he preferred to think deeply and investigate repeatedly. Only after completing the blueprint did he put it into practice. His ability to envision an enterprise's future was extraordinary. He knew that a flawed blueprint would affect the whole business.

## CH17-P010

### 中文原文

“在经营过程中，最为重要的是事前的准备和企划。如果是因为事先没有考虑周全，使得资金周转陷入窘境，销路受阻，那只能说是经营者缺乏能力。我本人在不动产方面蒙受损失，就是因为未能从一开始就‘经营’好。还有一点就是应该知道自己能力的范围，不应该超越这个范围。”

### English Translation

“The most important things in management are advance preparation and planning. If a failure to think matters through beforehand causes a cash-flow crisis or blocks sales channels, the manager can only be called incapable. I suffered losses in real estate because I failed to ‘manage’ properly from the beginning. One must also know the limits of one's abilities and never exceed them.”

## CH17-P011

### 中文原文

李秉哲说的是当时如果已经知道经营的真谛，就不会做那些冤枉的投资。他的理论是：如果一开始就没有筹划好资金的计划和销售的计划，那就不能称之为一个成功企业。

### English Translation

Lee meant that had he understood the essence of management at the time, he would not have made those misguided investments. His theory was that an enterprise could not be called successful unless its financing and sales plans were sound from the outset.

## CH17-P021

### 中文原文

这样看来，李秉哲具有水平的思考模式，而郑周永具有垂直的思考模式。水平和垂直的相交，呈现的就是牢固、美丽的三角形金字塔。

### English Translation

Seen this way, Lee possessed a horizontal mode of thought, while Chung possessed a vertical one. Where horizontal and vertical met, they formed a solid and beautiful triangular pyramid.

## CH17-P022

### 中文原文

韩国经济无法缺少李秉哲和郑周永正是因为他们的水平和垂直的结合。李秉哲通过生产生活用品，达到了水平层面上的大众化。反之，郑周永通过建筑业实现了结构上的空间化。这两个构造给韩国人的生活带来了翻天覆地的变化。

### English Translation

The Korean economy could not do without Lee and Chung precisely because horizontal and vertical combined in them. By producing daily necessities, Lee achieved popularization on the horizontal plane. Through construction, Chung created spatial structures. Together, these two structures transformed Korean life.
