### English Translation

### English Translation

Misfortune, however, soon struck. In 1937, Japan instigated the Marco Polo Bridge Incident in China, and full-scale war broke out between the two countries. Because a food-rationing system was to be introduced, every rice store in Korea was closed. At that point, Chung Ju-yung had been successfully operating Kyungil Rice Store for only two years. The blow was severe. The rice store he had labored over for two years was forced to close, but for the first time he had demonstrated through his own actions that honest work could lead to success.
