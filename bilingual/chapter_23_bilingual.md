### English Translation

### English Translation

In old age, however, Chung played by rules of his own making and could still sink nine shots each week. His golfing ability naturally became highly inconsistent after he turned eighty, but before then he had ranked among the finest golfers in the Korean business world. His sons Chung Mong-koo, Chung Mong-hun, and Chung Mong-yoon were all excellent golfers, and Chairman Chung Mong-hun was particularly renowned for hitting drives of three hundred yards.
