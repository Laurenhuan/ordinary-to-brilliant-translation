### CH01-P006

Source page: FB-P015

Source:

> 他出生于庆尚南道[^FB-F002]宜宁的地主李缵宇家，是李缵宇四个儿子中最小的一个。李秉哲的祖父李洪锡是当时的一位鸿儒，也是世代相传、家财万贯的地主。李秉哲出生于地主之家，在私塾学过一阵汉学后，就进入小学，中学接受正规的教育，后来又到日本的早稻田大学留学。

Source footnote:

> [^FB-F002]: 韩国的“道”相当于中国“省”一级的行政单位。

Translation:

Lee Byung-chull was born in Uiryeong, Gyeongsangnam-do[^FB-F002], into the family of Lee Chan-woo, a landowner, and was the youngest of four sons. His grandfather, Lee Hong-seok, was an eminent Confucian scholar of his time and a wealthy landowner whose family had held considerable property for generations. Born into a landowning family, Lee Byung-chull studied Chinese classics for a time at a traditional Korean village school known as a seodang before entering primary school and continuing his formal education through middle school. He later studied at Waseda University in Japan.

Translated footnote:

[^FB-F002]: In Korea, a “do” is an administrative division roughly equivalent to a province in China.

Review status: Approved for style baseline

### CH01-P014

Source page: FB-P015

Source:

> 李秉哲自五岁起便开始在祖父开设的文山亭书院学习《千字文》，五年间学了《资治通鉴》、《论语》等书。

Translation:

Beginning at age five, Lee Byung-chull studied *The Thousand-Character Classic* at Munsanjeong, a seodang founded by his grandfather. Over the next five years, he studied works including *Zizhi Tongjian* (*Comprehensive Mirror in Aid of Governance*) and *The Analects*.

Review status: Approved — human review round 2

### CH01-P015

Source page: FB-P015

Source:

> 郑周永的祖父也是私塾的教书先生。因此他从六岁起在祖父的私塾里读书，三年间学了《小学》、《大学》、《资治通鉴》、五言诗、七言诗等。得益于这段学习，郑周永在小学阶段已经没有太多可学的知识。

Translation:

Chung Ju-yung's grandfather also taught at a seodang. Beginning at age six, Chung studied at his grandfather's seodang for three years, reading *Elementary Learning*, *The Great Learning*, *Zizhi Tongjian*, and five-character and seven-character verse, among other works. There was little left for him to learn at the primary-school level.

Review status: Required revision applied — human review round 2

### CH01-P016

Source page: FB-P016

Source:

> 需要特别指出的是：尽管郑周永在私塾的学习只有短短的三年时间，但他所学的汉文《大学》、《资治通鉴》、五言诗、七言诗等，即便对于当今四年制本科大学毕业生而言，也是无法阅读和翻译的。

Translation:

It is worth emphasizing that although Chung Ju-yung spent only three short years at a seodang, the classical Chinese works he studied—including *The Great Learning*, *Zizhi Tongjian*, and five-character and seven-character verse—are texts that even today's graduates of four-year universities would be unable to read and translate.

Review status: Approved — human review round 2

### CH01-P018

Source page: FB-P016

Source:

> 姑且不说学问的多与少，仅就人情事理而言，《小学》和《大学》里就已经包含了大部分的内容。《资治通鉴》记载了泱泱中国1300年间历代皇室的历史。这本书与《通鉴节要》、《通鉴纲目》被朝鲜时代的读书人列为必读书目。

Translation:

Leaving aside the question of how extensive that learning was, *Elementary Learning* and *The Great Learning* alone cover much of what one needs to know about human relations and the ways of the world. *Zizhi Tongjian* chronicles the history of China's successive imperial houses over a span of 1,300 years. Scholars of the Joseon period regarded it, together with *Tongjian Jieyao* and *Zizhi Tongjian Gangmu*, as essential reading.

Review status: Approved — human review round 2
