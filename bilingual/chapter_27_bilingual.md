### English Translation

Most of the men who rendered distinguished service under the two giants, Lee Byung-chull and Chung Ju-yung, have now retired. ## CH27-P012

### 中文原文

李洙彬会长从首尔大学经济学系毕业后加入了三星旗下的第一制糖，然后自愿去了设在釜山的工厂，从此开始了作为一个三星人的生涯。他之所以自愿去地方工作，是为了熟悉工厂的操作流程。从去釜山后的第二年起，六年间他先后进入了会计、企划、管理部门工作，1970年起担任第一制糖的会计科科长这一要职。他不仅数学计算能力好，记忆力也相当出众，1972年被提拔到集团秘书室负责管理工作，在这里工作了四年。那段时期李洙彬在接受外部审计时，面对提问对答如流，连一个经营数值都不漏过，缜密的答辩让在场的审计人员都大为叹服，一时间传为佳话。他的记忆力和计算能力是何等出众由此可窥一斑。

### English Translation

After graduating from Seoul National University's Department of Economics, Lee Soo-bin joined Samsung affiliate Cheil Jedang and volunteered for its Busan plant, beginning his career as a Samsung man. He chose a regional posting to learn factory operations. Beginning in his second year in Busan, he spent six years rotating through accounting, planning, and management, and in 1970 assumed the important position of accounting-section chief at Cheil Jedang. Gifted in both calculation and memory, he was promoted in 1972 to a management role in the Chairman's Secretariat, where he worked for four years. During that period, when external auditors questioned him, Lee answered fluently and omitted not a single management figure. His meticulous replies deeply impressed the auditors and became a celebrated story, illustrating his exceptional memory and numerical ability.
