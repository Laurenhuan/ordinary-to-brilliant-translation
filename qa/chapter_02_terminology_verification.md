## Special Decision A — 丰田喜一郎

Candidate ID: `C2-T008`

Source locations: `CH02-P079`–`CH02-P081`

Status: `needs_human_decision`

### Verified external evidence

- Toyota official English uses **Kiichiro Toyoda**.
- Toyota official materials give Kiichiro Toyoda's dates as **1894–1952**.
- Toyota official history identifies Kiichiro Toyoda as the founder of Toyota Motor Corporation and the automotive-centered Toyota Group.
- Toyota official history identifies **Sakichi Toyoda** as Kiichiro's father, born in **1867**, and associates Sakichi with loom invention.

### Frozen-source evidence

The frozen Chinese source prints `丰田喜一郎（1867—1930）` and then supplies a carpenter-father, loom-invention, and life-story sequence. The printed name points to Kiichiro Toyoda, while the dates and major biographical details align substantially with Toyota's official account of Sakichi Toyoda.

### Verification conclusion

This is a **source-name / biographical-entity discrepancy**, not a routine romanization problem. It must not be resolved by automatically applying `Kiichiro Toyoda` to all three paragraphs, and the frozen source must not be corrected from external knowledge.

### Proposed human decision

The Project Lead should decide:

1. Which entity the English prose represents in P079–P081.
2. Whether to preserve the printed identity in translation with a translator/editor note.
3. Whether the note should explain that Toyota official records distinguish Kiichiro Toyoda (1894–1952), founder of Toyota Motor Corporation, from Sakichi Toyoda (1867–1930), the loom inventor and Kiichiro's father.
4. Whether first-reference role wording should be `founder of Toyota Motor Corporation`; concise later wording such as `Toyota founder` should be allowed only after the entity decision and only where it does not blur company history.

No English name is locked by this report.
