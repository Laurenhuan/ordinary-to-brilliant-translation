“Please do not worry. How could I go to Thailand for a project and return at a loss? I will be sure to come back having made money.”

## CH12-P115

### 中文原文

郑周永不认为自己失败。虽然期待的工程款泡汤，可是他获得的知识却是相当丰富的：急需提高和改进的地方是经验和技术。运用这些，他顺利地在韩国国内完成了庆釜高速公路的工程。

### English Translation

Chung did not regard himself as having failed. Although the contract revenue he had expected had evaporated, the knowledge he gained was considerable: the areas urgently requiring improvement were experience and technology. Applying what he had learned, he successfully completed the Kyungbu Expressway project in Korea.

## Notes

[^FB-F020]: Kyungbang is the abbreviated Korean name of the Kyungsung Spinning Company.

[^TN-CH12-001]: The source places both the Taehwa and Imjin rivers near Ulsan. The Taehwa River is in Ulsan, while the Imjin River is located in northern Korea and does not run through the Ulsan area.

[^TN-CH12-002]: The printed source contains the malformed phrase 韩国是如果掘地的; the translation renders the apparent intended meaning from context.
