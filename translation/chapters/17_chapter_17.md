Source page: FB-P163

Translation:

This was Lee's view of enterprise. As he said, before beginning anything he preferred to think deeply and investigate repeatedly. Only after completing the blueprint did he put it into practice. ### CH17-P010

Source page: FB-P163

Translation:

“The most important things in management are advance preparation and planning. If a failure to think matters through beforehand causes a cash-flow crisis or blocks sales channels, the manager can only be called incapable. I suffered losses in real estate because I failed to ‘manage’ properly from the beginning. One must also know the limits of one's abilities and never exceed them.”

### CH17-P011
