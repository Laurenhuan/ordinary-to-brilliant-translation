### CH24-P038

### CH24-P039

Source page: FB-P208

Translation:

To produce the finest fabric in the world, he began by selecting the wool itself. First he took the best-quality fibers, from the sheep's shoulders, then selected the finest strands one by one by hand. From wool that ordinarily measured 18.5 microns, he chose only the finest fibers, measuring 16.5 microns. From three tons of wool, Lee ultimately selected two hundred pounds, a process that took ten days. Choosing fine wool by the feel of one's fingers was arduous work.

Source page: FB-P208
