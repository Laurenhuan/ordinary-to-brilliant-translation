## CH05-P031

### English Translation

## CH05-P032

### English Translation

李秉哲在开始一项事业之前，一定要经过极为缜密细致的市场分析调查后再动手。而郑周永却是先动手、再奋斗的风格。

### English Translation

Before embarking on any venture, Lee Byung-chull always conducted an exceptionally meticulous and detailed market analysis before taking action. Chung Ju-yung, by contrast, would act first and then fight to make the venture succeed.

## CH05-P049

### 中文原文

如果说李秉哲是先想后做的话，郑周永就是先做后想。

### English Translation

If Lee Byung-chull planned first and then acted, Chung Ju-yung acted first and then thought.

## CH05-P050

### 中文原文

时至今日，郑周永和李秉哲两位巨人分别把现代和三星经营为世界性的大企业，我们无法说哪一种方法更具有优越性。

### English Translation

Today, the two titans, Chung Ju-yung and Lee Byung-chull, have respectively built Hyundai and Samsung into global enterprises, and it is impossible to say which approach is superior.

## CH05-P051

### 中文原文

在政府机关里看到从事建筑行业的人领走的钱比自己的要多出好多，回去之后当天便挂上现代土木建设的牌子，郑周永进入建筑业看来有些可笑，但是凭借他的直觉能够感觉到那是一个前景不错的产业。

### English Translation

Chung Ju-yung's entry into construction may seem almost comical: after seeing at a government office that people in construction were collecting far more money than he was, he returned that very day and put up a Hyundai Civil Works Company sign. Yet his intuition told him that the industry had excellent prospects.

## CH05-P052

### 中文原文

三星在开始新事业的时候，则是先制定一个计划，之后花时间和力气做成非常翔实的计划书，然后再开始动手。可是现代却是先铺摊子，之后再去赚钱。

### English Translation

When Samsung began a new business, it first drew up a plan, then spent time and effort producing an extremely detailed proposal before taking action. Hyundai, by contrast, first set up the operation and then went out to make money.

## CH05-P053

### 中文原文

近来所说的快速经营，即决断迅速，并能够快速进行下一步工作，这方面现代要领先于三星，并且在规模上要大于三星。但是实质方面，即经营成果、盈利结构等方面，三星要优于现代，这也是三星做决策前经过长期考察分析的缘故。

### English Translation

In what is now called speed-oriented management—the ability to decide swiftly and move quickly to the next step—Hyundai was ahead of Samsung and larger in scale. In substantive terms, however, including operating results and profit structure, Samsung was stronger, precisely because of the lengthy research and analysis it undertook before making decisions.

## CH05-P054

### 中文原文

这并不是在比较两个企业经营理念的优劣。可以说这是根据时代对企业经营理念的要求不同而决定的。

### English Translation

This is not a comparison of which company's management philosophy was better. Rather, the difference may be said to reflect what enterprise management required in different periods.

## CH05-P055

### 中文原文

如果说在硝烟弥漫的战场上和同类企业竞争厮杀需要的是现代所具有的冲劲，那么，要打创消费市场品牌，需要的却是对市场情况精密的分析和以此为基础得出对策的三星企业的模式。

### English Translation

If Hyundai's driving force is what a company needs when fighting its competitors on a smoke-filled battlefield, then building a brand in the consumer market requires Samsung's model: precise analysis of market conditions and strategies derived from that analysis.[^TN-CH05-005]

## CH05-P056

### 中文原文

如果说在 60 摄氏度高温下工地工作的工人，由于日晒中暑倒下，那么这个时候需要的就是指挥官快速的判断，而在销售电视、冰箱、电脑、半导体等商品的时候，即使是建立工厂的生产线也需要相当多的时间，之后还有对市场变化预测的要求，情况随时都有可能发生变化。

### English Translation

If workers laboring at a construction site in temperatures of 60 degrees Celsius collapse from heatstroke under the sun, what is needed is a commander's rapid judgment. But when selling televisions, refrigerators, computers, semiconductors, and similar goods, even setting up a factory production line takes considerable time, after which market changes must still be forecast—and circumstances may change at any moment.

## CH05-P057

### 中文原文

如今的现代集团正在苦苦奋战，如果不能重视自身的企业精神是非常危险的。

### English Translation

Today's Hyundai Group is engaged in a difficult struggle, and it would be dangerous for it to neglect its own corporate spirit.

## CH05-P058

### 中文原文

如果现代能够再次像在越南、中东那样出色完成工程，现代的企业精神就有可能重新成为评论的对象。

### English Translation

If Hyundai can once again execute projects as successfully as it did in Vietnam and the Middle East, Hyundai's corporate spirit may once more become a subject of discussion.

[^TN-CH05-001]: The source uses several Chinese forms for Samsung’s early trading entities. [^TN-CH05-002]: The printed source names the trading partner as “Channel 商会.” The historical identity of this business could not be independently verified in the sources reviewed for this project. “Channel Sanghoe” is therefore retained as a source-based form.

[^TN-CH05-004]: The printed source reads 收人 here, evidently a typographical error for 收入 (“income”). The translation renders the intended meaning as “income.”

[^TN-CH05-005]: The printed source reads 打创消费市场品牌, an apparently malformed phrase. The translation renders the evident intended sense as “build a brand in the consumer market.”
