### English Translation

By launching a misguided war, the country had reduced itself to rubble.

### English Translation

Today, Akasaka is one of Tokyo's best-known commercial districts, with clubs, bars, and restaurants glowing in neon at night, a city that never sleeps. At the time, however, it was little more than a desolate street.

## CH06-P026

### 中文原文

不仅如此。在日本，百年老店随处可见。长崎的蛋糕店文明堂生产一种蛋糕，保持了百年之久。在奈良的一家旅馆神堂屋于16世纪开业，迄今已经营业了400余年。在大阪的一家名为金刚组的寺庙建筑公司创于公元587年，到现在已经拥有1400年的历史，历经40代人，仍在从事着修建寺庙的工作。这家公司，据我所知是世界上历史最长的一家公司。如果前往东京的银座，会看到那里的银座百货商场。百年老店，汇聚了来自各地的商品。

### English Translation

Nor is that all. Century-old businesses can be found everywhere in Japan. Bunmeido in Nagasaki has produced the same kind of cake for a hundred years. The Shindoya inn in Nara opened in the sixteenth century and has operated for more than four hundred years. Kongō Gumi, an Osaka company specializing in temple construction, was founded in 587 and, after forty generations and some fourteen hundred years, continues to build temples. To my knowledge, it is the world's oldest company. In Tokyo's Ginza, meanwhile, one finds Ginza department stores—century-old establishments gathering merchandise from throughout the country.

## CH06-P027

### English Translation

Mitsui and Mitsubishi, the groups that represent Japan today, likewise have long histories. Mitsui Group was founded in 1876 and often takes pride in its century-long history. Mitsubishi Group was founded by Yataro Iwasaki in 1870 and has a history of more than 130 years.

## CH06-P028

### 中文原文

这些集团数百年不倒的原因何在？用一句话概括是：质量至上主义。为了生产出质量最优的产品，数百年，代代人都在苦心钻研。始终在为提高质量而不断努力。就连理发店都在代代相传，其他的行业更不必说。日本征服世界的动力正在于此：敬业精神。

### English Translation

What has enabled these groups to survive for centuries? In a single phrase: quality first. Generation after generation has devoted painstaking study to producing goods of the highest quality. They have worked ceaselessly to improve it. If even barbershops are passed down through generations, the same is all the more true of other trades. Here lies the driving force behind Japan's conquest of world markets: dedication to one's work.
