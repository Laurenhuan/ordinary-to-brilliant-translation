## Reserved Translator/Editor Notes

Exactly five Chapter 5 notes are reserved in textual order. Their wording is authoritative and must not be paraphrased.

### TN-CH05-001 — first relevant Samsung historical-entity occurrence (expected `CH05-P013`)

The source uses several Chinese forms for Samsung’s early trading entities. Ho-Am Foundation records that Samsung Moolsan Trading Company (삼성물산공사) was established in November 1948 and that Samsung Moolsan (삼성물산) was newly established in January 1951. This translation uses those verified period-specific English names where the referent is clear.

### TN-CH05-002 — `CH05-P032`

The printed source names the trading partner as “Channel 商会.” The historical identity of this business could not be independently verified in the sources reviewed for this project. “Channel Sanghoe” is therefore retained as a source-based form.

### TN-CH05-003 — first relevant Hyundai historical-company occurrence (expected `CH05-P037`)

Hyundai’s historical materials identify 현대자동차공업사 as “Hyundai Auto Service” and 현대토건사 as “Hyundai Civil Works Company.” The source uses varying Chinese forms for these early businesses; this translation follows the verified historical English names where the referent is clear.

### TN-CH05-004 — `CH05-P041`

The printed source reads 收人 here, evidently a typographical error for 收入 (“income”). The translation renders the intended meaning as “income.”

### TN-CH05-005 — `CH05-P055`

The printed source reads 打创消费市场品牌, an apparently malformed phrase. The translation renders the evident intended sense as “build a brand in the consumer market.”
